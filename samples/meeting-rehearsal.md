# Sample: clarify a delivery commitment

Original fictional training exercise prepared for this portfolio with AI assistance. Not client work and not evidence of a delivered paid engagement.

Audience: an English-speaking business meeting involving a supplier and customer.

Situation: A fictional supplier can deliver a prototype next month but cannot yet confirm the production date.

Task: Explain the distinction, ask what the customer needs for its planning, and agree who will confirm the next date.

Useful language:
- “We can commit to the prototype date. The production schedule still needs confirmation.”
- “Which decision depends on that date?”
- “I will confirm the schedule in writing after the technical review.”

Review questions: Was the commitment accurate? Was uncertainty clear? Was the next action assigned to a person and a date? Do not present this sample as a guarantee of commercial results.
