# Sample: a clearer customer update

New fictional writing/editing sample prepared with AI assistance. It is not a quotation from a client, published novel or Dr Brenda Flanagan's work.

Before: “We are looking into the thing you sent and hopefully will have something soon so please wait for our message.”

After: “We are reviewing the upload error you reported. We will send you a progress update by 15:00 tomorrow, even if the investigation is still open. Please keep the original file so we can compare it with our test.”

Editing rationale: name the issue, commit to an update rather than an unverified resolution, and give one useful next action. The deadline belongs only to this fictional exercise.
