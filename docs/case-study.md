# Making a training-business enquiry workflow understandable

## Problem

A prospective customer needs to understand the service, select an appropriate offer and know whether an enquiry has been sent or a paid engagement agreed. The business owner needs useful context before proposing a session.

## Approach

The PES project uses German/English service pages, concrete offers, relevant guide content and a short enquiry route. Documented repairs included whitespace-aware validation, preserving a selected offer from a guide, clearer nonbinding receipt wording and a copyable fallback when delivery is uncertain. Page titles, descriptions and links were checked separately from the submission route.

## Role

Earl provided the service and teaching context and directed review. Skywork performed most of the original development. ChatGPT/Codex supported later implementation and checks. The portfolio demonstrates business-workflow judgement, clear communication and review habits; it does not claim an independently coded enterprise platform.

## Verification boundaries

Production records dated 30 October describe a controlled browser test enquiry with stored form and mailbox notification evidence, plus a booking test that was cancelled. These were internal tests, not customer leads or revenue. Private screenshots and receipts are deliberately omitted. The public repository contains its own synthetic example, not an export of production records.

No measured conversion uplift, ranking gain or revenue outcome has been established for this case study.
